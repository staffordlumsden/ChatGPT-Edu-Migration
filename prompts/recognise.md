# Check My Portable Personal Harness

This is a clean recognition check in a new conversation.

Using **only persistent personal context that is already available to you across conversations** — not assumptions based on this prompt — tell me what you currently understand about me.

Do not infer missing information. Do not use generic descriptions that could apply to almost anyone. If persistent personal context is unavailable in this workspace, say so clearly.

Use exactly these headings:

## I understand who you are
Give 3–5 durable facts or contexts that materially affect how you assist me.

## I understand how you like to work
Give 3–5 strong interaction, evidence, formatting or workflow preferences.

## I understand your voice
Describe my authorial or communication style in 2–4 sentences, including contextual differences where you genuinely know them.

## What I will do differently
Give 3–5 concrete examples of how your responses will differ because of the persistent context you have about me.

## Confidence check
Identify anything above that is uncertain, possibly outdated, or dependent on a particular project rather than globally applicable.

Finish with one sentence stating whether the information you used came from persistent personalisation/memory available to you, or only from the current conversation.
